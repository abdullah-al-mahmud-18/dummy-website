Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

PomodoroTimer is a desktop timer and stopwatch app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your computer and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server and never receives your data.

What the app stores

For each session you record, the app stores its name, type (timer or stopwatch), mode (Work, Study, or Break), planned and actual duration, start and end time, and whether it was completed.

This is saved in a single file, pomodoro.db, on your computer:

  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PomodoroTimer\
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/PomodoroTimer/

The same folder also holds app log files and automatic backups of the database. These stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

Google Drive sync

Sync is optional. If you sign in with Google, the app uploads pomodoro.db to your own Google Drive and downloads it on your other devices, so they all show the same history.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission. This lets it see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • The file goes directly from your computer to your Google Drive. It does not pass through any server run by the developer.
  • The sign-in token that lets the app access Drive is saved on your computer in the app folder above. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.

PomodoroTimer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No accounts other than your own Google sign-in for sync.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No network access at all when sync is not set up.

Removing your data

  • To delete individual sessions, use the delete options on the History page.
  • To delete everything on a computer, delete the app folder listed above.
  • To delete the synced copy, delete pomodoro.db from your Google Drive and empty the trash.
  • To stop the app from accessing your Drive, remove PomodoroTimer from your Google Account connections.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the GitHub repository.
