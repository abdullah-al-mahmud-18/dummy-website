A simple timer and stopwatch for focused work.

Set a timer or start a stopwatch, tag the session as Work, Study, or Break, and see where your time went. Runs on Windows 11 and Ubuntu 24.04.

Download from GitHub

What it does

  • Timer with a progress bar, plus a desktop notification and sound when it finishes.
  • Stopwatch that counts up until you stop it.
  • Any duration you like, with a name for each session.
  • History of every session, with filters and delete.
  • Dashboard with totals and daily averages for Work, Study, and Break.

Your data

Your sessions are saved in a file on your computer. If you turn on sync, that one file is also saved to your own Google Drive so your other devices see the same history. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
